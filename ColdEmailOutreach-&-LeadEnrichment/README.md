This workflow automates the entire cold email outreach process starting from scraped leads. It first extracts leads from Apollo and filters them to ensure only valid single-email entries are processed. The cleaned data is stored in Google Sheets for tracking. Next, OpenAI generates a personalized outreach email based on your custom prompt. The email is then automatically sent using Instantly for outreach automation. Finally, the Google Sheet is updated with the generated AI email content for record-keeping and tracking.
